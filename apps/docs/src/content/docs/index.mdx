| [Inbox, Forms & Alert Banners](/strapi-plugin-inbox/)        | `inbox`          | Engagement             | Visual form builder, service-desk casework inbox, double opt-in subscribers, and site-wide emergency/announcement alert banners                |

  @vicacha-devs/strapi-plugin-events \
